# BNI Canvas — Fieldwork Studio
## Direction
Warm white workspace with a compact 218px left sidebar and simple breadcrumb header. Subtle terracotta accents, cream panels, and fine warm borders. Light login with route illustration on the left and form on the right.
## Palette
Ink #252525, paper #F6F5F2, surface #FFFFFF, line #E3E0DA, muted #94918C, burnt orange #BC572A, peach #E9B18B. Use one accent family with muted semantic statuses.
## Typography
Geist, headings with tight tracking and 30–48px scale. Body 13–15px. Numerals tabular. No serif or emoji.
## Layout
Compact persistent left navigation on desktop. Dashboard: asymmetric discovery panel and metrics, followed by an activity table and dark next-step panel. Search: compact horizontal controls, dominant map at left, results at right. Login: warm white canvas, illustrated route at left, form at right. Empty workspaces: oversized title column and divided content column.
## Pattern
Fine dot grid on discovery surfaces and schematic street grid on login. No gradients or decorative floating shapes.
## Responsive
Single-column layouts below 768px; sidebar becomes a slide-in menu. Search map stacks above results. Inputs and main actions at least 44px. Reduced motion supported.
## Honesty
Mock dashboard data remains explicitly marked. Placeholder workflows remain clear about feature availability.
